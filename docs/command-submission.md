# Submission, control buffers, and synchronization

## CONFIRMED — DDK host protocol

There are at least two distinct layers: the TA work CCB, of which KM knows the `SGXMKIF_CMDTA_SHARED` part, and the kernel command CCB for USE handlers. The comment itself says that the shared structure is only part of the complete TA command. [references/omap5-sgx-ddk-linux/eurasia_km/services4/include/sgx_mkif_km.h:153-201](../references/omap5-sgx-ddk-linux/eurasia_km/services4/include/sgx_mkif_km.h#L153); [references/omap5-sgx-ddk-linux/eurasia_km/services4/include/sgx_mkif_km.h:70-96](../references/omap5-sgx-ddk-linux/eurasia_km/services4/include/sgx_mkif_km.h#L70); [references/omap5-sgx-ddk-linux/eurasia_km/services4/srvkm/devices/sgx/sgxkick.c:79-96](../references/omap5-sgx-ddk-linux/eurasia_km/services4/srvkm/devices/sgx/sgxkick.c#L79).

## Kick path

1. **CONFIRMED:** the bridge resolves structures/handles and calls `SGXDoKickKM`; the KM checks whether the offset of the shared portion fits within the buffer. This is partial validation, not proof of validation of the entire stream. [references/omap5-sgx-ddk-linux/eurasia_km/services4/srvkm/bridged/sgx/bridged_sgx_bridge.c:188-605](../references/omap5-sgx-ddk-linux/eurasia_km/services4/srvkm/bridged/sgx/bridged_sgx_bridge.c#L188); [references/omap5-sgx-ddk-linux/eurasia_km/services4/srvkm/devices/sgx/sgxkick.c:79-96](../references/omap5-sgx-ddk-linux/eurasia_km/services4/srvkm/devices/sgx/sgxkick.c#L79).
2. **CONFIRMED:** fills in pending values and complete addresses, updates counters, and marks READY; then schedules `SGXMKIF_CMD_TA`. In the case of RETRY, it undoes the corresponding increments. [references/omap5-sgx-ddk-linux/eurasia_km/services4/srvkm/devices/sgx/sgxkick.c:120-256](../references/omap5-sgx-ddk-linux/eurasia_km/services4/srvkm/devices/sgx/sgxkick.c#L120); [references/omap5-sgx-ddk-linux/eurasia_km/services4/srvkm/devices/sgx/sgxkick.c:403](../references/omap5-sgx-ddk-linux/eurasia_km/services4/srvkm/devices/sgx/sgxkick.c#L403); [references/omap5-sgx-ddk-linux/eurasia_km/services4/srvkm/devices/sgx/sgxkick.c:732-803](../references/omap5-sgx-ddk-linux/eurasia_km/services4/srvkm/devices/sgx/sgxkick.c#L732).
3. **CONFIRMED:** the KM scheduler takes the power lock, requests the ON state, and calls the internal scheduler. [references/omap5-sgx-ddk-linux/eurasia_km/services4/srvkm/devices/sgx/sgxutils.c:677-732](../references/omap5-sgx-ddk-linux/eurasia_km/services4/srvkm/devices/sgx/sgxutils.c#L677).
4. **CONFIRMED:** ring availability is `((write+1)&255) != read`; it copies the command, incorporates invalidations, and resolves the handler. [references/omap5-sgx-ddk-linux/eurasia_km/services4/srvkm/devices/sgx/sgxutils.c:236-250](../references/omap5-sgx-ddk-linux/eurasia_km/services4/srvkm/devices/sgx/sgxutils.c#L236); [references/omap5-sgx-ddk-linux/eurasia_km/services4/srvkm/devices/sgx/sgxutils.c:442-498](../references/omap5-sgx-ddk-linux/eurasia_km/services4/srvkm/devices/sgx/sgxutils.c#L442). **INFERRED:** the scheme reserves one entry, allowing up to 255 entries to be in use.
5. **CONFIRMED:** advances write modulo 256, increments the shared event-kicker modulo 256, performs a write barrier, writes EVENT_KICK (or a conditional alternative branch), and performs a memory barrier. [references/omap5-sgx-ddk-linux/eurasia_km/services4/srvkm/devices/sgx/sgxutils.c:561](../references/omap5-sgx-ddk-linux/eurasia_km/services4/srvkm/devices/sgx/sgxutils.c#L561); [references/omap5-sgx-ddk-linux/eurasia_km/services4/srvkm/devices/sgx/sgxutils.c:608-638](../references/omap5-sgx-ddk-linux/eurasia_km/services4/srvkm/devices/sgx/sgxutils.c#L608).

**UNKNOWN:** the full meaning of the six words, VDM/TA/ISP stream formats, render target states, tile parameters, and the program that consumes the CCB. There is no basis for sending an invented “NOP” command or assuming that six zeros are harmless.

## Synchronization and completion

**CONFIRMED:** the protocol uses pending snapshots and GPU addresses for completion, with read/write and TA↔3D dependencies. In the generalized syncs branch, there are separate lists of TA sources/destinations and 3D sources. [references/omap5-sgx-ddk-linux/eurasia_km/services4/srvkm/devices/sgx/sgxkick.c:120-256](../references/omap5-sgx-ddk-linux/eurasia_km/services4/srvkm/devices/sgx/sgxkick.c#L120); [references/omap5-sgx-ddk-linux/eurasia_km/services4/include/sgx_mkif_km.h:169-195](../references/omap5-sgx-ddk-linux/eurasia_km/services4/include/sgx_mkif_km.h#L169).

**INFERRED:** an advanced read offset means consumption of the kernel CCB input, not completion of rendering; the existence of complete counters and separate TA/3D status provides the evidence. [references/omap5-sgx-ddk-linux/eurasia_km/services4/include/sgx_mkif_km.h:92-96](../references/omap5-sgx-ddk-linux/eurasia_km/services4/include/sgx_mkif_km.h#L92); [references/omap5-sgx-ddk-linux/eurasia_km/services4/include/sgx_mkif_km.h:169-199](../references/omap5-sgx-ddk-linux/eurasia_km/services4/include/sgx_mkif_km.h#L169). Wraparound, coherence, and translation to modern fences remain **UNKNOWN**.

**CONFIRMED:** the direct assignments to complete at the end of `sgxkick.c` are under `NO_HARDWARE`; they do not constitute a true completion algorithm. [references/omap5-sgx-ddk-linux/eurasia_km/services4/srvkm/devices/sgx/sgxkick.c:806-906](../references/omap5-sgx-ddk-linux/eurasia_km/services4/srvkm/devices/sgx/sgxkick.c#L806). Similarly, the host-side increment of read in `sgxutils.c` is conditioned by `NO_HARDWARE`. [references/omap5-sgx-ddk-linux/eurasia_km/services4/srvkm/devices/sgx/sgxutils.c:640-643](../references/omap5-sgx-ddk-linux/eurasia_km/services4/srvkm/devices/sgx/sgxutils.c#L640).

## IRQ and recovery

**CONFIRMED:** the TI ISR intersects status and enable, handles SW_EVENT, clears master/events, and samples the microkernel IRQ counter. The MISR examines HWR and active power. [references/omap5-sgx-ddk-linux/eurasia_km/services4/srvkm/devices/sgx/sgxinit.c:1809-1916](../references/omap5-sgx-ddk-linux/eurasia_km/services4/srvkm/devices/sgx/sgxinit.c#L1809). The recovery routine calls `SGXInitialise(..., true)`, not just an IRQ cleanup. [references/omap5-sgx-ddk-linux/eurasia_km/services4/srvkm/devices/sgx/sgxinit.c:1582-1645](../references/omap5-sgx-ddk-linux/eurasia_km/services4/srvkm/devices/sgx/sgxinit.c#L1582).

**CONFIRMED:** Linux Poulsbo installs a shared PCI IRQ, enables TWOD_COMPLETE and BIF_REQUESTER_FAULT, and only reads the blit status in the first case; it does not signal a 3D queue in this routine. [references/linux/drivers/gpu/drm/gma500/psb_irq.c:151-196](../references/linux/drivers/gpu/drm/gma500/psb_irq.c#L151); [references/linux/drivers/gpu/drm/gma500/psb_irq.c:278-325](../references/linux/drivers/gpu/drm/gma500/psb_irq.c#L278).

**UNKNOWN:** integration of SW_EVENT/EDM with the Poulsbo IRQ, timeout-bounded recovery, safe job discard, and BO lifetime after reset. Existing handlers do not provide this contract. See [Linux state](gma500-current-state.md).

## Transfer and 2D: partially known payload

**CONFIRMED:** `SGXSubmitTransferKM` checks the offset of the shared part and places the virtual address of the transfer context in `ui32Data[1]` before scheduling TRANSFER. The 2D path does the same with its context and schedules 2D. Therefore, this word has a known meaning for these operations; this does not reconstruct the pointed-to context nor the six words for all commands. [services4/srvkm/devices/sgx/sgxtransfer.c:104–110](../references/omap5-sgx-ddk-linux/eurasia_km/services4/srvkm/devices/sgx/sgxtransfer.c#L104); [services4/srvkm/devices/sgx/sgxtransfer.c:419–426](../references/omap5-sgx-ddk-linux/eurasia_km/services4/srvkm/devices/sgx/sgxtransfer.c#L419); [services4/srvkm/devices/sgx/sgxtransfer.c:563–570](../references/omap5-sgx-ddk-linux/eurasia_km/services4/srvkm/devices/sgx/sgxtransfer.c#L563); [services4/srvkm/devices/sgx/sgxtransfer.c:721–725](../references/omap5-sgx-ddk-linux/eurasia_km/services4/srvkm/devices/sgx/sgxtransfer.c#L721).

## Host watchdog

**CONFIRMED:** under `SUPPORT_HW_RECOVERY`, `SGXOSTimer` monitors EDM activity through registers indicated in `psDevInfo`, also compares the number of resets, and accumulates three samples without change before deciding on a lockup. There are exceptions for OpenCL delay and conditional workarounds; the path stops counting when the device is not powered. Upon detecting a lockup, it increments `ui32HostDetectedLockups` and calls recovery. It is not a universal three-millisecond timeout, nor proof of progress for each job. [services4/srvkm/devices/sgx/sgxinit.c:1662–1799](../references/omap5-sgx-ddk-linux/eurasia_km/services4/srvkm/devices/sgx/sgxinit.c#L1662). **UNKNOWN:** the EDM offsets and appropriate period for the Poulsbo stepping; TI integration does not validate them for Intel.
