# Submission, control buffers, and synchronization

## CONFIRMED — protocolo host do DDK

There are at least two distinct layers: the TA work CCB, of which KM knows the `SGXMKIF_CMDTA_SHARED` part, and the command CCB kernel for USE handlers. The comment itself says that the shared structure is only part of the complete TA command. [references/omap5-sgx-ddk-linux/eurasia_km/services4/include/sgx_mkif_km.h:153-201](../references/omap5-sgx-ddk-linux/eurasia_km/services4/include/sgx_mkif_km.h#L153); [references/omap5-sgx-ddk-linux/eurasia_km/services4/include/sgx_mkif_km.h:70-96](../references/omap5-sgx-ddk-linux/eurasia_km/services4/include/sgx_mkif_km.h#L70); [references/omap5-sgx-ddk-linux/eurasia_km/services4/srvkm/devices/sgx/sgxkick.c:79-96](../references/omap5-sgx-ddk-linux/eurasia_km/services4/srvkm/devices/sgx/sgxkick.c#L79).

## Caminho de kick

1. **CONFIRMED:** bridge resolves estruturas/handles and calls `SGXDoKickKM`; the KM checks if the offset of the shared portion fits in the buffer. This is partial validation, not proof of validation of the entire stream. [references/omap5-sgx-ddk-linux/eurasia_km/services4/srvkm/bridged/sgx/bridged_sgx_bridge.c:188-605](../references/omap5-sgx-ddk-linux/eurasia_km/services4/srvkm/bridged/sgx/bridged_sgx_bridge.c#L188); [references/omap5-sgx-ddk-linux/eurasia_km/services4/srvkm/devices/sgx/sgxkick.c:79-96](../references/omap5-sgx-ddk-linux/eurasia_km/services4/srvkm/devices/sgx/sgxkick.c#L79).
2. **CONFIRMED:** fills in pending values and complete addresses, updates counters, and marks READY; then schedules `SGXMKIF_CMD_TA`. In the case of RETRY, undoes the corresponding increments. [references/omap5-sgx-ddk-linux/eurasia_km/services4/srvkm/devices/sgx/sgxkick.c:120-256](../references/omap5-sgx-ddk-linux/eurasia_km/services4/srvkm/devices/sgx/sgxkick.c#L120); [references/omap5-sgx-ddk-linux/eurasia_km/services4/srvkm/devices/sgx/sgxkick.c:403](../references/omap5-sgx-ddk-linux/eurasia_km/services4/srvkm/devices/sgx/sgxkick.c#L403); [references/omap5-sgx-ddk-linux/eurasia_km/services4/srvkm/devices/sgx/sgxkick.c:732-803](../references/omap5-sgx-ddk-linux/eurasia_km/services4/srvkm/devices/sgx/sgxkick.c#L732).
3. **CONFIRMED:** scheduler KM toma power lock, solicita estado ON e chama scheduler interno. [references/omap5-sgx-ddk-linux/eurasia_km/services4/srvkm/devices/sgx/sgxutils.c:677-732](../references/omap5-sgx-ddk-linux/eurasia_km/services4/srvkm/devices/sgx/sgxutils.c#L677).
4. **CONFIRMED:** ring availability is `((write+1)&255) != read`; copies command, incorporates invalidateidations, and resolves handler. [references/omap5-sgx-ddk-linux/eurasia_km/services4/srvkm/devices/sgx/sgxutils.c:236-250](../references/omap5-sgx-ddk-linux/eurasia_km/services4/srvkm/devices/sgx/sgxutils.c#L236); [references/omap5-sgx-ddk-linux/eurasia_km/services4/srvkm/devices/sgx/sgxutils.c:442-498](../references/omap5-sgx-ddk-linux/eurasia_km/services4/srvkm/devices/sgx/sgxutils.c#L442). **INFERRED:** the scheme reserves one entry, allowing up to 255 unused.
5. **CONFIRMED:** advances write modulo 256, increments shared event-kicker modulo 256, performs write barrier, writes EVENT_KICK (or conditional alternative branch) and performs memory barrier. [references/omap5-sgx-ddk-linux/eurasia_km/services4/srvkm/devices/sgx/sgxutils.c:561](../references/omap5-sgx-ddk-linux/eurasia_km/services4/srvkm/devices/sgx/sgxutils.c#L561); [references/omap5-sgx-ddk-linux/eurasia_km/services4/srvkm/devices/sgx/sgxutils.c:608-638](../references/omap5-sgx-ddk-linux/eurasia_km/services4/srvkm/devices/sgx/sgxutils.c#L608).

**UNKNOWN:** full meaning of the six words, VDM/TA/ISP stream formats, render target states, tile parameters, and program that consumes the CCB. There is no basis for sending an invented “NOP” command or assuming that six zeros are harmless.

## Synchronization and completion

**CONFIRMED:** the protocol uses pending snapshots and GPU addresses for completion, with dependencies on read/write and TA↔3D. In the generalized syncs branch there are separate lists of sources/destinations TA and 3D sources. [references/omap5-sgx-ddk-linux/eurasia_km/services4/srvkm/devices/sgx/sgxkick.c:120-256](../references/omap5-sgx-ddk-linux/eurasia_km/services4/srvkm/devices/sgx/sgxkick.c#L120); [references/omap5-sgx-ddk-linux/eurasia_km/services4/include/sgx_mkif_km.h:169-195](../references/omap5-sgx-ddk-linux/eurasia_km/services4/include/sgx_mkif_km.h#L169).

**INFERRED:** advanced read offset means consumption of the CCB kernel input, not completion of rendering; the existence of complete counters and separate TA/3D status is the evidence. [references/omap5-sgx-ddk-linux/eurasia_km/services4/include/sgx_mkif_km.h:92-96](../references/omap5-sgx-ddk-linux/eurasia_km/services4/include/sgx_mkif_km.h#L92); [references/omap5-sgx-ddk-linux/eurasia_km/services4/include/sgx_mkif_km.h:169-199](../references/omap5-sgx-ddk-linux/eurasia_km/services4/include/sgx_mkif_km.h#L169). Wraparound, coherence, and translation to modern fences remain **UNKNOWN**.

**CONFIRMED:** the direct assignments of complete at the end of `sgxkick.c` are under `NO_HARDWARE`; they do not constitute a true completion algorithm. [references/omap5-sgx-ddk-linux/eurasia_km/services4/srvkm/devices/sgx/sgxkick.c:806-906](../references/omap5-sgx-ddk-linux/eurasia_km/services4/srvkm/devices/sgx/sgxkick.c#L806). Similarly, the increment of read by the host in `sgxutils.c` is conditioned by `NO_HARDWARE`. [references/omap5-sgx-ddk-linux/eurasia_km/services4/srvkm/devices/sgx/sgxutils.c:640-643](../references/omap5-sgx-ddk-linux/eurasia_km/services4/srvkm/devices/sgx/sgxutils.c#L640).

## IRQ e recovery

**CONFIRMED:** ISR TI intersects status and enable, attends SW_EVENT, clears master/eventos and samples the IRQ counter of the microkernel. MISR examines HWR and active power. [references/omap5-sgx-ddk-linux/eurasia_km/services4/srvkm/devices/sgx/sgxinit.c:1809-1916](../references/omap5-sgx-ddk-linux/eurasia_km/services4/srvkm/devices/sgx/sgxinit.c#L1809). The recovery routine calls `SGXInitialise(..., true)`, not just an IRQ cleanup. [references/omap5-sgx-ddk-linux/eurasia_km/services4/srvkm/devices/sgx/sgxinit.c:1582-1645](../references/omap5-sgx-ddk-linux/eurasia_km/services4/srvkm/devices/sgx/sgxinit.c#L1582).

**CONFIRMED:** Linux Poulsbo installs shared PCI IRQ, enables TWOD_COMPLETE and BIF_REQUESTER_FAULT, and only reads the blit status in the first case; it does not signal a 3D queue in this routine. [references/linux/drivers/gpu/drm/gma500/psb_irq.c:151-196](../references/linux/drivers/gpu/drm/gma500/psb_irq.c#L151); [references/linux/drivers/gpu/drm/gma500/psb_irq.c:278-325](../references/linux/drivers/gpu/drm/gma500/psb_irq.c#L278).

**UNKNOWN:** integration of SW_EVENT/EDM with the Poulsbo IRQ, recovery limited by timeout, safe discard of jobs and BO lifetime after reset. Existing handlers do not provide this contract. See [estado Linux](gma500-current-state.md).

## Transfer and 2D: partially known payload

**CONFIRMED:** `SGXSubmitTransferKM` checks the offset of the shared part and places the virtual address of the transfer context in `ui32Data[1]`, before scheduling TRANSFER. The 2D path does the same with its context and schedules 2D. Therefore, there is a known meaning for this word in these operations; this does not reconstruct the pointed context nor the six words for all commands. [services4/srvkm/devices/sgx/sgxtransfer.c:104–110](../references/omap5-sgx-ddk-linux/eurasia_km/services4/srvkm/devices/sgx/sgxtransfer.c#L104); [services4/srvkm/devices/sgx/sgxtransfer.c:419–426](../references/omap5-sgx-ddk-linux/eurasia_km/services4/srvkm/devices/sgx/sgxtransfer.c#L419); [services4/srvkm/devices/sgx/sgxtransfer.c:563–570](../references/omap5-sgx-ddk-linux/eurasia_km/services4/srvkm/devices/sgx/sgxtransfer.c#L563); [services4/srvkm/devices/sgx/sgxtransfer.c:721–725](../references/omap5-sgx-ddk-linux/eurasia_km/services4/srvkm/devices/sgx/sgxtransfer.c#L721).

## Watchdog host

**CONFIRMED:** under `SUPPORT_HW_RECOVERY`, `SGXOSTimer` monitors EDM activity through registers indicated in `psDevInfo`, also compares the number of resets, and accumulates three samples without change before deciding on lockup. There are exceptions for OpenCL delay and conditional workarounds; the path stops counting when the device is without power. Upon detecting lockup, it increments `ui32HostDetectedLockups` and calls recovery. It is not a universal three-millisecond timeout, nor proof of progress for each job. [services4/srvkm/devices/sgx/sgxinit.c:1662–1799](../references/omap5-sgx-ddk-linux/eurasia_km/services4/srvkm/devices/sgx/sgxinit.c#L1662). **UNKNOWN:** EDM offsets and the appropriate period for Poulsbo stepping; TI integration does not validate them for Intel.
