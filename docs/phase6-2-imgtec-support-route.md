# Imagination documentation and support route

Imagination’s public forum stated in 2013 that hardware TRMs were exposed to graphics-IP licensees, while the SGX Architecture Guide for Developers was public; the same response invited hardware questions through the forum or support ([P6-2-001](evidence-matrix.csv)). Earlier SGX535 discussion was redirected to private developer support ([P5-003](evidence-matrix.csv)).

These sources show that an authorized support route historically existed. They do not show that an SGX535 TRM is public, that present-day support retains Poulsbo material, or that it may disclose licensee documentation.

## Legitimate request scope

An inquiry can ask whether Imagination can provide a written, attributable answer for the following narrow Series5 properties:

- access attributes and read effects of `CORE_ID` and `CORE_REVISION`;
- preconditions for their access: power, clocks, reset, firmware/MMU/BIF state;
- revision and BRN coverage; and
- behaviour and recovery expectations when the block is unavailable.

Poulsbo power integration may belong to Intel rather than Imagination. The public forum explicitly distinguished SGX535 from SGX545 and directed Intel-platform questions to the platform provider ([P5-004](evidence-matrix.csv)). An Imagination answer therefore needs an explicit platform scope before it can affect a Poulsbo gate requirement.

No inquiry was sent in Phase 6.2.
