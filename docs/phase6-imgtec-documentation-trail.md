# Imagination SGX535 documentation trail

Phase 6 rechecked the public trail found in Phase 5. The material below identifies documentation and support boundaries; it does not provide a register contract.

- An Imagination developer-forum thread records a user-supplied filename, `Eurasia.3D Input Parameter Format.1.3.37a.SGX535 1.2.External.pdf`, followed by a staff direction to contact developer support ([P5-003](evidence-matrix.csv)). The public posts do not authenticate the filename, establish its license, or disclose its contents.
- In a separate Intel-platform discussion, Imagination staff says SGX535 and SGX545 have feature differences and directs platform-specific questions to Intel ([P5-004](evidence-matrix.csv)). This supports keeping core and platform evidence separate.

No public, attributable SGX535 or Series5 manual found in this phase explicitly marks `CORE_ID` or `CORE_REVISION` as read-only, states that reads have no side effects, or defines their required power, clock, reset, revision, errata, failure, or recovery conditions on Poulsbo.

**INFERRED:** detailed SGX material was distributed through developer-support channels. That inference describes the public access trail only. It does not establish that a particular private document contains the missing contract.
