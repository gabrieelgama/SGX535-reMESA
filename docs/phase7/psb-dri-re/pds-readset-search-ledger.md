## Comparative result

The three emitters that store `0x07000345` all initialize dwords `+0x00`, `+0x04`, and `+0x20` before it. The clear and frozen-primary emitters leave the same nine slots without a producer store. The texture-replace emitter adds data at four of those slots *and* two later instructions. A plausible hypothesis is that `0x07000345` reads only the three common initialized dwords, but these samples are not a controlled ISA experiment: they cannot exclude a read of an unchanged unwritten slot, nor distinguish a read by the first versus later instructions. The hypothesis remains **INFERRED**; the exact read set remains **UNKNOWN**.

The first missing fact is an SGX535-applicable decode of `0x07000345`'s source selectors and data-store addressing. An authenticated instruction reference, a permissively licensed independently implemented decoder with demonstrated SGX535 applicability, or equivalent direct evidence would decide whether `+0x08…+0x1f` and `+0x24…+0x2f` are consumed. Initializing those slots to zero without that fact would change an unknown input, not reproduce a proven output. No hardware probe is authorized by this search.
