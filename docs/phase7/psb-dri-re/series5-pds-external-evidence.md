# Series5 PDS selector rule: external-evidence result

**Decision (2026-09-25): read set UNKNOWN; FG-02 remains open.** This pass sought an independently usable Series5/SGX535 rule for the selected `0x07000345` and `0xaf000000`. It found no source that both defines their operand/read behavior and has established applicability and acceptable provenance. This is a bounded statement about the sources and indexes below, not a claim that a public rule cannot exist. The prior [42-row retained corpus](pds-instruction-corpus.csv), [CPU-side differential constraints](pds-comparative-encoding.md), and [hard boundary](selected-pds-hard-boundary.md) remain authoritative. No retained corpus extraction was repeated.

## Bit constraints still supported

The selected word is `0x07000345`, with set bits `{0,2,6,8,9,24,25,26}`. The analogous retained word `0x07042345` adds exactly bits 13 and 18 (`XOR 0x00042000`). CPU-side formulas for other retained PDS-shaped classes shift indices into those positions, and builders move the initialized triplet from `+00/+04/+20` to `+08/+0c/+28`. Those are **CONFIRMED CPU emissions** and an **INFERRED operand-index correlation**. No new public field definition has confirmed that the `0x45` class uses the same field meanings, has exactly three dword reads, or has no implicit/other source. The mixed-program `0x070b0345` still refutes the prior two-index formula as a complete decoder: its `0x000b0000` difference includes bits not explained by that formula. No new evidence assigns opcode, condition, mode, bank or destination meanings to them.

The selected 56-byte record has data at `+00,+04,+20`, with producer-unwritten dwords at `+08,+0c,+10,+14,+18,+1c,+24,+28,+2c`; the instruction words are at `+30=0x07000345` and `+34=0xaf000000`. The interleaved CPU layout labels are DS0[0..7] for `+00..+1c` and DS1[0..3] for `+20..+2c`. **None of the nine holes is proved dead.** The outbuf can reuse a BO without clearing it, so their contents must not be represented as zero.

The favored but still **INFERRED** model reads `{+00,+04,+20}` in the first instruction and no data in the terminal. Three competing models remain observationally compatible with every retained CPU store: (1) `0x07000345` also reads at least one hole; (2) its apparent index bits select a condition/mode or another operand layout; (3) `0xaf000000` reads implicit or data-store state, including a hole. The `0xaf000000` standalone four-byte emission favors a terminal/no-local-data interpretation but proves neither instruction class nor absence of implicit reads. Its high byte `0xaf` and zero low 24 bits are literal bit observations only.

**Smallest missing artifact:** a legitimately usable SGX535-applicable Series5 PDS definition for the `0x45` instruction's source count, source-index and bank selectors, plus the `0xaf` instruction's source behavior. A narrowly scoped official answer to those fields could suffice; a whole manual is not necessary. An independently authored, licensed decoder would need explicit compatible-core provenance and agreement with `0x07000345`, `0x07042345`, counterexample `0x070b0345`, `0xaf000000`, and other relevant corpus rows before its read set could be accepted. A decoder limited to one matching word is insufficient.

FG-02 therefore remains **OPEN**. No read-set promotion, clean-room PDS program, or hardware gate change follows from this search. Gate B is **BLOCKED**, hardware whitelist `[]`, and hardware functionality **UNVERIFIED**. No binary was executed, no ioctl/MMIO/GPU action occurred, and no source under `references/` was changed.
